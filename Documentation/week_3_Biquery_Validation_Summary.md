Week 3 – Event Count by Activity

Objective

To analyse the distribution and frequency of patient activities in the CareFlow dataset using SQL.

Work Completed

- Executed SQL queries using "GROUP BY" and "COUNT()" to aggregate event records by activity.
- Calculated the total number of events for each hospital activity.
- Applied "ORDER BY" to sort activities in descending order of event count.
- Identified the most and least frequently recorded activities.

Key Results

Metric| Result
Total event records| 3,351
Distinct activities| 8
Most frequent activity| Doctor Consultation
Doctor Consultation events| 609
Least frequent activity| Follow-up
Follow-up events| 97

Outcome

Successfully analysed the distribution of patient events across eight hospital activities. The results provide a clear overview of activity frequency, highlighting Doctor Consultation as the most frequently recorded activity and Follow-up as the least frequent.

This analysis establishes a foundation for further exploration of patient journey patterns and hospital workflow efficiency.
